Why the carton can lose

A carton at ₹35/unit vs loose at ₹40 looks like a flat ₹5 win. But a 24-unit carton selling 6/week is four weeks of locked capital plus spoilage — which can erase the gap. Four inputs, a break-even, not a ratio you can eyeball.

True cost/usable unit = carton price ÷ (1 − spoilage) + a capital-holding charge over the weeks it takes to sell down. Break-even off-take is the weekly speed where carton finally beats loose.
